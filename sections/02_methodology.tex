\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:methodology}

This systematic literature review is conducted in adherence to the established guidelines for evidence-based software engineering formulated by Kitchenham and Charters \cite{kitchenham2007guidelines}. The methodology encompasses search strategy planning, study selection via explicit inclusion/exclusion criteria, data extraction, and thematic synthesis.

\subsection{Search Strategy and Databases}
We queried four major electronic academic databases indexing high-impact software engineering literature: IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect (Elsevier), and SpringerLink. Supplementary backward and forward snowballing was conducted on prominent seminal surveys and conference proceedings (e.g., IEEE RE, REFSQ, WER).

The search query was engineered around three conceptual facets:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Facet 1 (Intervention):} (\texttt{"prototyping"} OR \texttt{"prototype"} OR \texttt{"mock-up"} OR \texttt{"wireframe"} OR \texttt{"paper prototype"});
    \item \textbf{Facet 2 (Domain/Phase):} (\texttt{"requirements elicitation"} OR \texttt{"requirements validation"} OR \texttt{"requirements engineering"});
    \item \textbf{Facet 3 (Outcome/Phenomenon):} (\texttt{"missing requirements"} OR \texttt{"unclear requirements"} OR \texttt{"ambiguity"} OR \texttt{"completeness"} OR \texttt{"tacit knowledge"}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria}
To ensure methodological rigor and relevance to the research questions, studies were filtered according to the following explicit criteria:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Inclusion Criteria (IC):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{IC1:} Peer-reviewed journal articles, conference papers, or workshop proceedings published in reputable software engineering venues.
        \item \textit{IC2:} Empirical investigation (controlled experiments, quasi-experiments, case studies, or mapping studies) examining the application of prototypes in requirements discovery or validation.
        \item \textit{IC3:} Direct evaluation of requirement quality attributes, specifically regarding missing requirements detection, ambiguity resolution, or requirement categorization.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Exclusion Criteria (EC):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{EC1:} Publications where prototyping refers solely to rapid software code implementation or rapid architecture without requirements engineering stakeholder involvement.
        \item \textit{EC2:} Short vision papers ($< 4$ pages), extended abstracts, non-peer-reviewed white papers, or tertiary editorial overviews.
        \item \textit{EC3:} Studies lacking transparent empirical data collection or systematic analytical methodology.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Corpus Profile and Selection Results}
Following deduplication, title/abstract screening, and full-text quality appraisal, a finalized corpus of 17 studies was established, comprising:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{10 Core Primary Studies:} Published predominantly between 2020 and 2025 in top-tier journals (e.g., \textit{Empirical Software Engineering}, \textit{Information and Software Technology}, \textit{Requirements Engineering}) and leading conferences, providing direct empirical measurements on RQ6.1, RQ6.2, and RQ6.3 \cite{bjarnason2023empirically, rueda2020requirements, uv2023empirical, rej2022evolve, antonelli2022iterative, bano2023videos, gupta2022strategic, acm2022proposal, diaz2021gender, cascon2025ambiguity}.
    \item \textbf{7 Seminal / Theoretical Anchor Studies:} Providing theoretical foundations for participative evaluation, ambiguity measurement, elicitation maturity, and video/persona mediums \cite{hickey1998prototyping, abad2018loud, jserd2019protopersona, davis2018maturity, ferrari2016ambiguity, spoletini2018interview, brill2010videos}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Data Extraction and Synthesis Protocol}
For each included publication, data was extracted using a structured extraction instrument structured across five operational dimensions:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textit{Bibliographic Metadata:} Authors, year, venue, study type (case study, controlled experiment, field study).
    \item \textit{PAM Categorization:} Prototype purpose, scope, fidelity/medium, operational exploration, and stage of use \cite{bjarnason2023empirically}.
    \item \textit{Defect Discovery Metrics:} Qualitative and quantitative evidence regarding the volume of elicited requirements, percentage of missing requirements discovered, and ambiguity reduction rates.
    \item \textit{Requirement Typology:} Functional (FR), Non-Functional (NFR), Usability/GUI, and domain constraints.
    \item \textit{Analytical Triad Mapping:} Explicit answers to the mentor's \textit{Why}, \textit{How}, and \textit{Classification} criteria.
\end{enumerate}
The extracted evidence was consolidated into the cross-study synthesis matrix, forming the empirical foundation for thematic and comparative analyses.
