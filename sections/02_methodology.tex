\section{Review methodology}
\label{sec:methodology}

This review follows standard evidence-based software engineering guidelines by Kitchenham and PRISMA reporting principles. To ensure comprehensive coverage, the study combines systematic database queries with backward snowballing starting from core benchmark papers in the domain.

\subsection{Search strategy and electronic databases}
Our search strategy connects two complementary sources: (1) foundational seed papers identified from seminal RE literature (such as the PAM model \cite{bjarnason2023empirically} and empirical benchmark experiments \cite{rueda2020requirements}), and (2) systematic searches across four primary electronic databases: IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect, and SpringerLink. Supplementary snowballing was applied to reference lists to ensure theoretical completeness.

The search string combined three core facets using Boolean operators:
(1) \textit{Intervention}: \texttt{"prototyping"}, \texttt{"prototype"}, \texttt{"wireframe"}, \texttt{"mock-up"}, \texttt{"paper prototype"};
(2) \textit{RE Phase}: \texttt{"requirements elicitation"}, \texttt{"requirements validation"}, \texttt{"requirements discovery"}; and
(3) \textit{Target Defect}: \texttt{"missing requirements"}, \texttt{"unclear requirements"}, \texttt{"ambiguity"}, \texttt{"tacit knowledge"}.

The resulting corpus of 26 studies is organized into two complementary tiers:
(1) \textit{15 Core Primary Empirical Studies}, delivering quantitative and qualitative evidence addressing RQ6.1, RQ6.2, and RQ6.3 across controlled experiments, case studies, and automated tools \cite{bjarnason2023empirically, rueda2020requirements, uv2023empirical, rej2022evolve, antonelli2022iterative, bano2023videos, gupta2022strategic, acm2022proposal, diaz2021gender, cascon2025ambiguity, hoffmann2011automotive, lending2022rubric, kolthoff2024interlinking, kolthoff2024selfelicitation, wei2023boosting}; and
(2) \textit{11 Foundational Studies}, providing theoretical baselines for IREB principles, participative evaluation, ambiguity classifications, and agile requirements practices \cite{pohl2016requirements, hickey1998prototyping, abad2018loud, jserd2019protopersona, davis2018maturity, ferrari2016ambiguity, spoletini2018interview, brill2010videos, lim2008anatomy, inayat2015systematic, berry2003contract}.

\subsection{Quality appraisal and data extraction}
Each candidate study was assessed across four quality benchmarks: clear research objectives, rigor of empirical design, objective defect measurement, and discussion of validity threats. Data was systematically extracted into a standardized matrix capturing PAM dimensions (purpose, scope, media, exploration strategy, use) \cite{bjarnason2023empirically}, empirical findings ($p$-values, effect sizes, defect counts), and requirement categories.
